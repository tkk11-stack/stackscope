Arabia 24h Avec: The Everyday Plate Finnish Households Actually Use

The "24h" in the name is the point: this is Arabia's everyday line, built for a plate you'll use morning, noon, and night rather than one you save for company. The Avec pattern — a dense field of small dashes radiating out from the center — reads as texture more than decoration, which is why it works equally well under a fried egg or a plated dinner.

At 20cm, it's sized for a light meal or a side plate alongside a larger dinnerware set, and the glaze is tough enough to handle the daily dishwasher-microwave cycle that "fine china" usually can't survive. It's the kind of plate that disappears into your routine instead of demanding to be treated carefully.

The blue colorway is the most recognizable, but Arabia produces Avec in several finishes, so it's worth checking current options if you're trying to match an existing set or start one in a specific color.
