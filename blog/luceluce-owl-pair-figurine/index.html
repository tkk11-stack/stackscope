This Black & White Owl Pair Is an Entryway Fixture, Not Just a Figurine

Owl figurines carry symbolic weight across a lot of design traditions — wisdom, protection, good fortune — which is part of why they show up so consistently as entryway and doorstep decor rather than being tucked away on a random shelf. This pairing of one white and one black owl leans into a modern Scandinavian palette instead of the more folksy, painted-ceramic look owl decor is often associated with.

Sold as a matched pair, the two pieces are designed to flank a doorway, sit on either end of a console table, or bookend a mantel — visual symmetry does a lot of the "finished room" work on its own.

The minimal color palette makes this an easy match for existing Nordic-style decor rather than something that needs to be built around.
