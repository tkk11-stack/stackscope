An Abstract Bulldog Canvas Is a Pet Portrait That Doesn't Look Like One

It comes finished on an internal wooden frame, ready to hang without extra framing costs, and the abstract treatment means it holds its own next to other modern art pieces instead of looking like the "pet corner" of an otherwise stylish room.
