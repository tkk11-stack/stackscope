Marimekko Siirtolapuutarha: The Plate Version of Finland's Most Famous Print

Siirtolapuutarha — named after Finland's allotment garden communities — is one of Marimekko's most recognizable prints: dense clusters of small round blooms rendered in bold, graphic outlines. It's better known on textiles, but the plate version translates the print's flatness and contrast surprisingly well onto ceramic.

At 20cm, this sits in side-plate territory, which is actually the right size for a print this busy — a full dinner plate in this pattern can compete with the food on it, while a smaller plate lets it work as an accent alongside plainer pieces.

Marimekko's ceramics carry the same practical glaze standards as their better-known textiles are decorative: dishwasher and microwave safe, meant to be used rather than displayed behind glass. If you already own Marimekko textiles, this is the easiest way to bring the same visual language to the table without redecorating a room.
