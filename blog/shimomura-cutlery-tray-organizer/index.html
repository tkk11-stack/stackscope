A Sliding Cutlery Tray That Actually Fits Japanese-Sized Drawers

Cutlery trays imported from larger kitchens often overshoot the drawer they're meant for, leaving a gap that just collects loose stray items anyway. Shimomura's two-tier tray is built to the shallower, narrower drawer depth common in Japanese kitchens, which also makes it a good fit for smaller kitchens anywhere else.

The clever part is the sliding second tier: it extends over the first without lifting out, so you get two full layers of divided storage in the vertical space one tray would normally use — spoons and forks on top, less-frequently-used serving pieces or chopsticks underneath.

At 313 reviews and counting, it's a proven, unglamorous workhorse rather than a design piece, which is exactly what a drawer organizer should be. It's resin, dishwasher-safe, and comes in a few finishes if you want it to disappear into the drawer rather than stand out.
