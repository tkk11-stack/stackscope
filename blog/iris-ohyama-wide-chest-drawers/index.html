Iris Ohyama's Wide Chest: Furniture-Grade Storage That Still Ships Flat

Iris Ohyama is one of Japan's largest home goods manufacturers, and this wide three-drawer chest is a good example of why: it's sized like real bedroom furniture (about 53cm wide, 59cm tall) but is made almost entirely of frosted plastic, so it's a fraction of the price and weight of a comparable wood chest — and it still ships and assembles at home rather than arriving fully built.

The frosted drawers let you see roughly what's inside without a full reveal, which is a small but genuinely useful feature for anyone who's ever opened three identical drawers looking for one sweater. A plastic top plate gives it a slightly more finished look than a bare drawer stack, so it can stand on its own in a bedroom or entryway instead of needing to be tucked out of sight.

At this size, it's a legitimate furniture replacement for a closet or small bedroom — not just a bin, but a piece you'd actually leave visible.
